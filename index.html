Reuben's Specialties:

UX Research

Mixed-methods research end to end: usability tests, contextual inquiry, RITE rounds, surveys and benchmarks that drive product decisions at HP, Amazon and Adobe.

AI Product Research

Studying how people trust, use and evaluate AI: generative AI search on Fire TV, AI design critique in Adobe Creative Cloud, and AI features in HP software.

Research Systems

Building the instruments that make research repeatable: standardized study templates, calibration anchors, automated analysis and a cross-study knowledge base.

Published Research

First author on HCI papers about AI and UX: a taxonomy of generative AI interfaces, data presentation preferences, and multimodal LLMs as UI judges.
