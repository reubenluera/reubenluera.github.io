## The one-paragraph brief

The site exists to get a UX research hiring manager past the recruiter screen. The 2026-10-08
diagnosis was that recruiters matched on the LinkedIn title "AI UX Researcher" but the portfolio
read as a designer and intern: no text headline, HP work absent, six specialties, intern-labelled
links, a lead case study whose body was images. The current site fixes the framing. The biggest
remaining gap is content: there are still no HP case studies. See [[backlog]].
