Class names are Framer hashes and will not change unless the site is regenerated, so they are
safe to reference.

## Home page, top to bottom

1. **Nav** (`Navigation 4` component). Fixed position. Phone variant uses `.nav-phone-links`
   (hand-written, in `base.css`) for Resume and LinkedIn; they are forced white.
2. **Hero** `.nMbd5OdMxj > .nc6zq_F9zd.before-after`. Two stacked images, `ba-left` (colour layer)
   and `ba-right` (sketch layer, clipped by `--pos`). An invisible `input[type=range]` covers the
   whole box and drives `--pos`. The handle is `.ba-line::after` (circle) and `.ba-line::before`
   (the two arrows), positioned at `bottom: 9%` so it sits on the sweater, not the face. There is
   no load-time animation and no hint label, by decision.
3. **Headline** `.hero-intro` (hand-written). `h1` is "Reuben Luera", the paragraph leads with
   "AI UX Researcher at HP." Max width 1080 with 40px padding so the text aligns with the section
   headings at 140px on a 1280 viewport. On phones it gets `margin-top: 28px`.
4. **Project Highlights** `.nIWrqHTkji`. Four cards in **two separate flex containers**:
   `.nYycZgH4dD` (row 1: MLLM judge, Live Guide) and `.ng4mm69FI1` (row 2: Data Delivery, Fire TV
   Search). Both must be 1000px wide and centred at every breakpoint. At 810 to 1199px the cards
   are `flex: 1 1 0` and fill their row, which is why the widths matter.
5. **Specialties** `.nu819LNN5w > .nJ7aakRI2A`, a 2 by 2 grid. Four cards since 2026-10-08:
   UX Research, AI Product Research, Research Systems, Published Research. The decorative circle
   and starburst lines (`.nnXgXTR6Uy`, `.nAUUtwLbwq`) are siblings of the cards and stay.
6. **Related Links** `.nNu7WDnfiU` carousel. Six `Card 2` instances in a `.carousel-track`. Each
   card's image is bound to its class name, so reorder by moving whole `.inst` blocks. Order:
   arXiv survey paper, CNET, Live Guide article, GEM Fellows, Adobe Life spotlight, Friends of
   Figma panel. Each card's figure `aria-label` names its own title.
7. **Footer** (`Footer` component), three variants.

## Case study page anatomy (mllmjudge as the model)

- Title `h1`, subtitle `h2`, a ticker of paper thumbnails, "Link to full Paper".
- `PROJECT SUMMARY` grid of label and value pairs.
- `.glance` section with `What happened` bullets (hand-written styles in `base.css`).
- Alternating gradient band headings and content blocks. Each content block is
  `div > div.rt > h2` followed by an `a[role=img]` figure whose image is a CSS background.
- `.prose` paragraphs (hand-written) sit between the heading and the figure. 18px Nunito on
  desktop, 16px on phones.
- `DESIGN RECOMMENDATIONS` block with three numbered items.
- `.proj-nav` previous and next links, then the footer.

## Figures and `assets/figures.css`

Case study figures are `a` or `div` elements with `role="img"` and a CSS `background-image`.
The Framer export gives each a box whose shape changes per breakpoint and rarely matches the
image, with `background-size: cover`, so images were cropped (top and bottom at tablet widths,
sides on phones). `assets/figures.css` is generated by `tools/fix_figures.py` and linked after
`base.css` on every page. It pins all 85 content figures to their image's aspect ratio with
`contain`, emitted inside the same media queries the page uses because a box can be
width-driven at one breakpoint and height-driven (`height: 100%; width: auto`, the filmstrip
rows on the Live Guide page) at another. Slideshow slides keep the frame height and take width
from the ratio. Home-page card art and the paper-thumbnail tickers are left alone. Never edit
`figures.css` by hand; change the generator and re-run it, then bump `figures.css?v=`.

## Hand-written CSS in `assets/base.css`, in order

Globals and `.rt` reset, before/after slider, carousel, slideshow, `.glance` outcome summary,

`hero-overlay` branch only, `.hero-wrap` and `.hero-overlay`.
